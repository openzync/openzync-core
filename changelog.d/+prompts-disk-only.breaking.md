BREAKING: Prompt templates are disk-only. The `GET/PUT/POST/DELETE /v1/admin/org/prompts*` endpoints (list, get, set, versions, rollback, set-default, system, import) are removed and the `prompt_templates` table is dropped — any custom per-org template edits are lost. Prompts now resolve from `services/worker/prompts/manifest.yaml` + `.jinja2` files cached at startup; change defaults via git. The custom-instructions endpoints and `extraction_schemas.prompt_template` overrides are unaffected.
