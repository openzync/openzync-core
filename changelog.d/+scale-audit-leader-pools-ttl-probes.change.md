Fix multi-replica stampedes and tighten scale budgets: worker cron registration, platform seed, and Ollama prewarm are now leader-guarded via a Redis `SET NX EX` lock (one leader runs crons/prewarm with jittered retry; followers skip with a log); seed inserts use `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` so losing the insert race is a no-op. DB pool defaults drop to 4+1 (75 conns at max Helm replicas, inside Postgres 100) and `FALKORDB_MAX_CONNECTIONS` (20) is now the single canonical pool size — per-request shadow pools reuse it and refuse to fork a second pool on the system URL. `observations:pending:*` markers get a 1h TTL, failed-job results re-expire to 4h (was 24h), SurrealDB connections get a 10s connect timeout with 30m idle eviction and a 32-entry LRU cap, and the worker `/ready` now checks Postgres + Ollama alongside Redis. Embedder calls are capped at 8 in flight with one backoff retry on 429/5xx and split connect/read timeouts; FalkorDB pools gain a 5s connect timeout; failed Redis-memory alerts and `/ready` blackbox guidance are documented in `alerts.yml` and compose.
